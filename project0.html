Part 1

Pictures of my friend at varying focal lengths. I shot these on an iPhone 16 Plus, starting close-up with 0.5x zoom, and stepping back while keeping his face centered, until I reached 10x zoom, taking photos at each step. As seen above, his face begins heavily distorted, but slowly becomes more proportional as the zoom increases.

Part 2

Above are my close/far photos of a building facade, specifically of the Doe library on campus. I found that the close photo revealed more depth, and appeared less "flat" than the far away photo. Specifically, this is most apparent in the middle of the photo (right above the door), where the columns in the far photo appear flat, and the same depth as the windows behind them. In comparison, in the close picture, the columns have more apparent curvature, and seem to be visually separated from the windows behind them.
